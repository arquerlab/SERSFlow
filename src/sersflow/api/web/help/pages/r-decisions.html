Which method should I use?

Rules of thumb for the choices you meet most often. They are starting points: always confirm with the “After:” previews.

Baseline method

Your dataTryWhy
Smooth fluorescence background, sharp bandsasls or arplsTwo knobs (lam, p); robust default
Strongly curved, changing backgroundP-spline variants (pspline_*), airplsMore flexible
You want no tuningsnip, mor / mormolOnly a window size, about the widest peak in points
Gentle slope onlymodpoly / imodpoly order 2–3Simple, predictable
XPS core levelshirley (static) or an active Shirley in the fitPhysical step background

Check with After: baseline. If the baseline cuts into peaks raise lam; if it floats above the bottom, lower p or raise lam.

Smoothing

  • Noise is not hurting your integrals or fits? Skip smoothing. Smoothing broadens peaks.
  • Needed for peak finding (nearest_peak) or derivatives: window about a third of the narrowest peak, polyorder 2–3.

XPS background: static or active?

  • Active (inside the fit) when you use recipes or fit several overlapping peaks; the background is optimised with the peaks.
  • Static baseline step when you want the same background subtraction for everything, or a Tougaard shape.
  • Never both.
